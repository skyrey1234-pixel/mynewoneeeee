# Skyrey Vision Master Pack

A Codex/Manus-ready strategy and build workspace for the Skyrey creative and business universe.

## Core idea

Skyrey is not building disconnected careers. He is building one universe with multiple entrances:

**Music creates attention → Streaming creates connection → AI creates revenue and ownership → Fashion creates culture → Stories create legacy.**

The phases are not rigid. They overlap and reinforce each other.

## Start here

1. Read `MASTER_VISION.md`.
2. Read `PLAN.md` for the execution order.
3. Give Codex the contents of `prompts/CODEX_MASTER_PROMPT.md`.
4. Give Manus the contents of `prompts/MANUS_MASTER_PROMPT.md`.
5. Open `site/index.html` in a browser to preview the starter vision website.

## Folder map

- `docs/` — complete strategy, phases, business model, content system, ownership, and roadmap.
- `ai-products/` — product briefs for the music identity engine and halftime sports analysis platform.
- `prompts/` — reusable prompts for Codex, Manus, websites, pitches, music rollout, and investors.
- `site/` — a working no-build starter website.
- `data/vision.json` — structured data for apps, dashboards, websites, or AI agents.
