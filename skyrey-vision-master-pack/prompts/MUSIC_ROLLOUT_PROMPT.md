# Jud Is Us Rollout Prompt

Act as a music growth strategist, creative director, TikTok content planner, and live activation producer.

Build a 12-week rollout for **Jud Is Us**.

For each priority song, create:

- Five viral clip candidates
- Three dance or participation concepts
- Three visual identities
- Creator seeding targets
- DJ outreach plan
- Silent-disco activation
- Content calendar
- Measurement plan

Do not assume virality. Build a testing system that uses retention, rewatches, shares, saves, comments, and sound uses to identify what to scale.
