# Investor / Strategic Partner Pitch Prompt

Create a concise, credible pitch from the Skyrey vision files.

Audience: [INSERT AUDIENCE]
Desired outcome: [INSERT ASK]
Time limit: [INSERT LENGTH]

The pitch must:

- Lead with one clear thesis
- Explain why the ventures reinforce each other
- Show existing proof without exaggeration
- Separate current products from future concepts
- Explain revenue models
- Emphasize ownership and IP
- End with a specific ask

Avoid presenting every idea equally. Prioritize the opportunity most relevant to the audience.
