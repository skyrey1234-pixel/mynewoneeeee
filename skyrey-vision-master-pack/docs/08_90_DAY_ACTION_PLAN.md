# 90-Day Action Plan

## Month 1 — Define and package

### Music

- Choose 1–3 priority songs from **Jud Is Us**
- Identify 5 candidate viral moments per song
- Define visual identity
- Create content templates
- Build creator and DJ outreach list

### AI

- Select one flagship AI product
- Create a demo
- Write the offer
- Create a one-page landing page
- Define pilot pricing

### Brand

- Launch or refine one central website
- Create an email capture
- Organize all assets and IP

### Story

- Create the *Nine of Them* story bible
- Establish a weekly writing target

## Month 2 — Test and sell

### Music

- Publish consistent short-form tests
- Track retention, shares, saves, and sound use
- Start creator seeding
- Run live DJ or crowd tests

### AI

- Pitch 10–25 qualified buyers
- Offer a paid or tightly scoped pilot
- Gather feedback
- Turn results into a case study

### Streaming

- Pilot 3 show formats
- Select the strongest recurring concept
- Build guest pipeline

## Month 3 — Double down

### Music

- Scale the best-performing sound and format
- Produce higher-quality versions of winning content
- Capture real-world reactions

### AI

- Convert pilot into monthly revenue
- Standardize onboarding
- Prepare enterprise materials

### Brand

- Publish founder story and master vision
- Begin documentary-style recurring content

### Story

- Complete a meaningful first section or chapter milestone

## Weekly operating rhythm

- 2 focused music sessions
- 2 content production blocks
- 2 AI sales/build blocks
- 1 streaming session
- 1 *Nine of Them* writing block
- 1 executive review
