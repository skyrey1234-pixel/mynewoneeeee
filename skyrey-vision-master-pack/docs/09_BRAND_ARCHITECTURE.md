# Brand Architecture

## Recommended model

### Parent company

**RT Global** or **Ray Trinidad Global**

Purpose: ownership, strategy, partnerships, investment, and management.

### Public creator brand

**Skyrey**

Purpose: artist identity, interviews, streaming, public story, community, and cultural influence.

### Technology division

**Skyrey Labs** or **RT Global AI**

Purpose: AI products, enterprise solutions, consulting, SaaS, research, and licensing.

### Music division

**Skyrey Music**

Purpose: releases, publishing, performances, collaborations, music IP, and artist development.

### Media division

**Skyrey Studios**

Purpose: streaming, film, television, animation, documentaries, podcasts, and visual production.

### Publishing/story division

Name to be determined.

Purpose: *Nine of Them*, books, audio experiences, story bibles, characters, and adaptation rights.

### Fashion division

Name to be determined.

Purpose: designer collections, runway, collaborations, e-commerce, and physical brand expression.

## Brand rule

Every division should have its own identity, but all should clearly connect to the same founder vision.
