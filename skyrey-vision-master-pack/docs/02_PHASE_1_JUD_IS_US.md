# Phase One — Jud Is Us: Music as the Ignition Point

## Objective

Turn **Jud Is Us** into a participatory movement rather than simply releasing an album.

The project should live in:

- TikTok dances
- Rave edits
- House and club environments
- Silent discos
- College parties
- DJ sets
- Festival content
- Fashion edits
- Emotional “main character” videos
- Nightlife and transformation content

## Strategic idea

Every priority song needs a recognizable social behavior attached to it.

Possible behaviors:

- Signature dance move
- Group movement on the drop
- Hand gesture
- Transition format
- Outfit transformation
- Rave-light visual cue
- Call-and-response lyric
- Emotional confession format
- “POV” scenario

## Viral content testing

Before deciding the official viral moment, test multiple 10–20 second clips.

For each song, create:

1. Performance clip
2. Dance attempt
3. Lyric-on-screen version
4. Rave visual version
5. Silent-disco concept
6. Story-behind-the-song clip
7. Outfit or transformation edit
8. Crowd simulation or reaction concept

The winning clip is selected by evidence:

- Completion rate
- Rewatches
- Shares
- Saves
- Sound uses
- Comments quoting lyrics
- People asking for the release

## Creator seeding

Begin with the right micro-communities, not only celebrities.

Targets:

- Dancers
- Rave creators
- DJs
- College creators
- Nightlife pages
- Fashion creators
- Festival creators
- Fitness and movement creators
- Visual editors
- Local Miami and Jacksonville creators

The first goal is 20–50 aligned creators who can make the sound look alive.

## Community behavior

When people use the sound:

- Repost strong videos
- Comment quickly
- Make reaction videos
- Duet or stitch selected posts
- Create tutorials
- Credit originators
- Give fans a feeling of authorship in the movement

## Offline validation

Take the strongest song into real rooms:

- DJ tests
- Silent discos
- College events
- Small clubs
- Pop-up performances
- Listening events

Capture crowd reactions. Those clips become proof for larger promoters, radio, playlists, festivals, and collaborators.

## Core message

Do not market the project as:

> Please listen to my song.

Market it as:

> This is the sound everybody is about to be moving to.
