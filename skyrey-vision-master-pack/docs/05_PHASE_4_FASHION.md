# Phase Four — Designer Fashion Expansion

## Objective

Create a legitimate fashion label, not ordinary artist merchandise.

## Brand standard

The clothing should be desirable even to someone who does not know Skyrey’s music.

It should have:

- Original silhouettes
- Signature pieces
- Premium storytelling
- Strong styling
- Quality construction
- Recognizable visual identity
- Distinctive campaigns

## Creative connection

The fashion can express themes from **Jud Is Us** and the larger universe:

- Nightlife
- Freedom
- Emotion
- Rave culture
- Futurism
- Technology
- Spiritual symbolism
- Transformation
- Individuality
- Rebellion
- Beauty

## New York expansion

New York can become the fashion and media expansion point through relationships with:

- Designers
- Pattern makers
- Manufacturers
- Stylists
- Photographers
- Models
- Creative directors
- Buyers
- Publications
- Investors
- Artists

Skyrey has discussed collaborating with a designer named Jesus who appeared on *Project Runway*. Before formalizing any partnership, verify role, expectations, ownership, compensation, and creative control in writing.

## First launch

Begin with a focused capsule, not a huge catalog.

Potential pieces:

- Signature jacket
- Premium top
- Distinctive pants
- One accessory
- Limited runway piece

## Content strategy

Document:

- Sketches
- Fabric selection
- Design meetings
- Fittings
- Production
- Campaign creation
- Casting
- Runway preparation
- Launch day

## Core line

> The music is what people hear. The clothing is how people become part of the world.
