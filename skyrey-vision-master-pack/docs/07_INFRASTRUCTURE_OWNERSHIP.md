# Infrastructure, Ownership, and Protection

## Parent company

Create one umbrella that makes the vision understandable.

Possible structure:

- RT Global / Ray Trinidad Global — parent company
  - Skyrey Music
  - Skyrey Studios
  - Skyrey Labs
  - Fashion label
  - Publishing and story IP
  - Film/television/animation division

Names should be checked for availability before final use.

## Intellectual property checklist

Track and protect:

- Artist name
- Album titles
- Brand names
- Logos
- Music masters
- Music publishing
- Recordings
- App names
- Source code
- Product designs
- Trade secrets
- Datasets
- Characters
- Manuscripts
- Screenplays
- Story bibles
- Fashion designs
- Visual assets

Use professional legal guidance for trademarks, copyrights, contracts, licensing, and company formation.

## Documentation system
