# Product Brief — Halftime AI Coach

## Problem

Coaches have limited time during halftime to review footage, identify patterns, and communicate adjustments.

## Solution

A video-analysis assistant that organizes observations and relevant clips into a clear halftime report.

## Possible outputs

- Formation tendencies
- Repeated concepts
- Explosive plays
- Missed assignments
- Coverage indicators
- Pressure patterns
- Player tendencies
- Tagged clips
- Suggested questions for coaching review

## Positioning

The system does not replace coaches. It gives staff organized information faster.

## MVP workflow

1. Upload or ingest video
2. Extract frames/clips
3. Detect and tag play segments
4. Produce play-by-play observations
5. Generate halftime summary
6. Link claims to clips
7. Export report

## Future features

- Live sideline workflow
- Telestration overlays
- Team-specific terminology
- Opponent scouting
- Player development reports
- Broadcast analysis

## Commercial model

- Per game
- Monthly team subscription
- Annual program license
- Enterprise deployment
