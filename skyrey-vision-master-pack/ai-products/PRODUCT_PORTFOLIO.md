# AI Product Portfolio

## Flagship candidates

### 1. Artist Identity Music Engine

Helps artists create faster while preserving their tone, emotional identity, themes, and creative direction.

Primary customers:

- Independent artists
- Producers
- Studios
- Labels
- Management companies

Potential model:

- Subscription
- Studio license
- Label enterprise license

### 2. Halftime AI Coach

Analyzes recorded or live game footage and organizes useful patterns, clips, and observations for halftime review.

Primary customers:

- High schools
- Colleges
- Professional teams
- Training facilities
- Sports organizations

Potential model:

- Per-game pricing
- Team subscription
- League or enterprise license
