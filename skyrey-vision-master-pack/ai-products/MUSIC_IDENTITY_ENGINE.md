# Product Brief — Artist Identity Music Engine

## Problem

Artists often use AI tools that generate generic results or weaken the artist’s identity.

## Solution

An artist-controlled system that learns approved creative signals and assists with new work while preserving identity.

## Possible inputs

- Previous songs
- Lyrics
- Themes
- Emotional tone
- Approved references
- Vocal qualities
- Production preferences
- Visual identity

## Possible outputs

- Song concepts
- Lyric directions
- Melody ideas
- Arrangement ideas
- Production briefs
- Visual concepts
- Release content

## Guardrails

- Artist approval required
- Clear rights and permissions
- Transparent use of source material
- No unauthorized voice cloning
- Secure private workspaces

## Positioning

> AI should extend the artist, not erase the artist.

## MVP

1. Artist profile setup
2. Upload approved reference material
3. Identity summary
4. Creative brief generator
5. Song concept workspace
6. Exportable session brief
